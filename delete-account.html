Account and Data Deletion

Request deletion in the app or without installing it.

Updated 7 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-06

Inside Campus Match

Open Settings > Delete Account. Read the confirmation and choose Delete Account to submit the request. Keep the app open until it reports success or an error. Sign-out alone does not delete an account.

Without the app

Email campusmatch2026@gmail.com from the email used for Campus Match with the subject “Campus Match account deletion”. We will verify ownership before acting. Do not include your password, M-Pesa PIN or an identity document. You can also use that address to ask to access, correct or delete specific personal data.

What is removed or retained

The current deletion flow removes profile photos and Campus Moment media, redacts the payment phone number and raw provider request/callback data, and deletes the authentication account and data covered by the account’s deletion relationships. Published event listings and event images may remain without your account link; contact support if they identify you. Minimal financial records may remain for accounting, disputes, refund handling or fraud review, for 5 years after the end of the year of the payment, or longer if a dispute, tax matter or legal case requires it. Backups may persist until the provider’s normal expiry.

Previously downloaded or screenshotted content held by another person is outside the service’s deletion control. Expired signed media links can remain usable until the link expiry. Contact support if deletion fails or you believe information is still visible after a successful request.

Contact Campus Match

Independent student social service for adults aged 18 or older. Kenya. Not an official Maseno University service.

Support, privacy, refund and deletion requests: campusmatch2026@gmail.com.

Request account deletion by email

Your email app opens. A request is sent only when you send the email. Do not include your password or M-Pesa PIN.
