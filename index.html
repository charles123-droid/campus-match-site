For Maseno University students · 18+

Meet students on your campus

Campus Match helps Maseno students find dates, friends, study buddies and people to learn new skills from. It is free to download and use.

Download for AndroidHow to install

Android only for now (APK, version 35). The Google Play version is on its way. There is no iPhone version yet.

What you can do

  • Dating. Swipe through profiles, match when you both like each other, and chat with your matches.
  • Friends, study buddies and networking. Choose what you are looking for, so you meet the right people.
  • Campus Moments. Share a photo with your matches for 24 hours, and reply to theirs.
  • Campus events. See what is on, mark the events you attend and collect campus badges.
  • Skill Swap. Offer something you are good at and learn something new from another student.
  • Nearby. If you turn it on, see how far other students are. Your exact location is never shown.

How to install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above and wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This is needed for any app that does not come from the Play Store.
  3. Tap Install, then open Campus Match.
  4. Sign up with your email, confirm you are 18 or over, and set up your profile.

Already have an older version? Install the new one over it. Your account and matches stay.

Safety comes first

  • 18 and over only. Accounts we believe belong to someone under 18 are restricted.
  • Report and block. Report or block someone from their profile, and report a Moment or a Skill Swap user. A moderator checks every report, and child safety reports are checked first.
  • Private photos. Photos are shown to other users through private links that expire, not public web addresses.
  • Your choice. You decide what you are looking for and whether to use Nearby. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Read the Child Safety Standards and the Privacy Policy.
