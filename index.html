Who this policy covers

Campus Match is operated by Charles Okindo, an individual based in Kenya. Campus Match is an independent student social app for adults aged 18 or older. It is not an official Maseno University service. Privacy, support and data requests: campusmatch2026@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android app, its web version and this policy website.

Account and optional profile information

Account creation uses an email address, password, account identifier and a declaration that you are at least 18 and accept the Terms. Supabase Authentication processes credentials. Passwords are not stored as plain text in profile records.

Profile information can include a display name, campus, gender, age, course, year of study, biography, interests, discovery preferences and a photo. Course, year, biography, age and interests are optional. Choose “Prefer not to say” for gender if you do not wish to disclose it. Do not put an identity document, private address or other confidential information in your profile.

Details you choose to put on a discoverable profile are visible to other users permitted to see that profile. Adding personal profile information is your choice. You can edit or remove optional details and pause Dating discovery.

Your gender, dating preferences and dating activity can reveal your sex or sexual orientation, which the law treats as sensitive. We use them for Dating and Nearby only with your consent, to show you to people and suggest matches. Without consent you stay hidden from both and can still use other features. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time. Withdrawing hides you from both from then on and does not affect earlier use.

Activity and visibility

The service stores likes, passes, Super Likes, matches, match messages, icebreaker answers, this-or-that choices, poll votes, event interests and attendance confirmations, earned badges, Skill Swap offers, requests, messages, session arrangements, completion confirmations, feedback and safety reports. It also stores likes on Campus Moments and in-app notifications, including whether you have read them.

Matched users can see each other’s Campus Moments. Moments stop appearing after 24 hours. Expired media is queued for periodic cleanup; a previously issued signed media link may continue to work until its own expiry. Removing a match or blocking someone prevents new access checks from authorizing that person.

If you reply to a Moment, your reply is saved as a match message, labelled as a reply to that Moment. The other person can see it in your chat, and it stays there after the Moment ends. The photo preview in the chat stops showing once the Moment can no longer be viewed.

People who can view your Moment can like it. You receive an in-app notification naming the person who liked it, and you can see who liked it while the Moment is available. Other viewers do not see that list or the like count. Removing a like hides the notification. Likes are deleted when the Moment’s record is cleaned up, but a notification about a like can stay in your list after the Moment ends. Blocking someone hides their likes and notifications from you.

If you report a Moment, the report records your account, the reported account, your chosen reason, the Moment’s identifier, its posting time and a copy of its caption. Approved moderators can also view the reported photo. The photo is kept for review for up to 30 days, even if the Moment has ended or its owner has deleted it, unless all reports about it are closed sooner or the owner’s account is deleted. Other users cannot see it during that time. The text copy can remain with the report after the photo is removed.

Match messages are for the participants. Authorized operators may access service data to operate the app, investigate reports, respond to requests or comply with law. Messages are not advertised as end-to-end encrypted. Other users may save or screenshot content they can view; deletion cannot remove copies they made outside the service.

Optional permissions and payments

Photo access is requested when you choose to upload an image. Location access is requested for optional Nearby discovery while using the app. Coordinates are rounded before storage and approximate distances are displayed. Nearby visibility expires after 24 hours; use “Stop Sharing My Location” to clear saved coordinates sooner. No background location access is requested.

Optional local event reminders use notification permission. In-app notifications, such as Moment likes, do not need that permission and are not sent to your phone when the app is closed. You can decline or revoke device permissions. A system permission prompt is separate from agreeing to the Terms.

M-Pesa purchases use the number you enter, the quoted amount, transaction references, provider results and receipt information. If you enter another person’s number, that person’s phone receives the prompt and the number is stored with the payment. Campus Match never asks for your M-Pesa PIN. Premium access, free-trial use and daily Super Like usage are stored to enforce purchased terms and prevent duplicate grants. If payment-confirmation SMS is enabled, the SMS provider receives your number and confirmation text; provider setup or delivery can fail.

Providers, storage and purposes

Supabase supplies authentication, database, storage and server functions. Safaricom Daraja/M-Pesa processes payment requests. Google Play processes purchases made in the Google Play version of the app, and we receive the order and purchase details. Expo supplies app builds and installed Expo modules support device features. Africa’s Talking is the optional payment-confirmation SMS provider. This public policy site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Support email is handled through Google Gmail. Providers may process data outside Kenya; contact us for questions about the providers and transfer arrangements.

We use information to provide accounts and the features you request, fulfill purchases, protect accounts, moderate reports and meet applicable obligations. Optional profile, location, media and reminder features are initiated by you. Acceptance of the Terms is not permission for unrelated marketing. No advertising or optional analytics SDK was identified in the reviewed app source. No advertising trackers are added by this policy website. Hosting and service providers can receive technical request/security logs.

The app saves sign-in sessions, theme preferences and feature state on the device, including which Campus Moments you have already viewed. The web version uses browser storage for sign-in. This policy site stores only your dismissal of its storage notice in the current browser session. See the Cookie and Storage Policy for the distinction between native-app storage and browser storage.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account and feature records are retained while needed for the service and the purpose for which they were supplied. Moment visibility and Nearby visibility have the shorter periods described above. In-app notifications are kept while your account exists, although the app shows only your latest 50. We do not promise that every stored record or provider backup disappears immediately when a screen changes.

Use Settings > Delete Account to request deletion from inside the app. The current deletion flow removes your stored profile photos and Campus Moment media, redacts the phone number and raw M-Pesa payloads, and deletes the authentication account and records linked by deletion rules. Published event listings and event images may remain without your account link. Ask support to review any event content that identifies you. Minimal financial records may remain for accounting, fraud review or refund handling. These keep details such as the amount, date, product, reference and receipt, without your phone number or account link. We keep payment records for 5 years after the end of the year of the payment, or longer if a dispute, tax matter or legal case requires it, and then delete them. If someone who liked your Moment deletes their account, their like is removed and a notification may remain without their name. Provider backup copies may remain until their normal expiry. A failed deletion displays an error so you can retry or contact support.

Without the app, email campusmatch2026@gmail.com with the subject “Campus Match account deletion” from the account email. We may ask for enough information to verify ownership. Never send a password, M-Pesa PIN or identity document by ordinary email. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, objection or other applicable data rights. You may also raise a complaint with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Children and policy updates

Campus Match does not offer children’s accounts. Do not register if you are under 18. If you believe an account belongs to a child, use in-app reporting or contact support so it can be reviewed. Self-declared age and email verification are not proof of identity or university enrollment.

Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy version. We cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or that other users will comply with the community rules.
