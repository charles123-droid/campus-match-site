Eligibility and agreement

Campus Match is operated by Charles Okindo, an individual based in Kenya. Campus Match is an independent service for adult students. You must be at least 18 to register or use its dating, matching and social features. Creating an account requires an explicit age declaration and agreement to these Terms. Existing accounts must confirm their age and accept the current policies before continuing. Do not create an account for a child. We may restrict an account we reasonably believe belongs to someone under 18 and ask for more confirmation of age. We ask for an identity document only when nothing less will do. Campus Match is not endorsed by or operated by Maseno University.

Keep your sign-in details private. Use truthful information and only content you are entitled to share. An “Email verified” badge means confirmation of the account email, not verification of identity, age, university enrollment, qualifications or safety.

Community and content rules

Do not harass, impersonate, discriminate, threaten, scam, spam, share sexual content involving minors, exploit another person, or post unlawful content. Do not publish another person’s private information or copyrighted work without permission. You must have the rights or permission needed for photos and other content you upload.

You retain rights in your content. You authorize Campus Match and its service providers to store, process and display it only as needed to deliver the features and visibility choices you use. You can report content, block users and unmatch. Report immediate danger to the appropriate local emergency services.

Skill Swap participants arrange their own sessions. Meet in a public place and use the reporting tools if needed. Feedback must describe a real exchange. Event interest counts measure saved interest, not guaranteed attendance. Badges describe app activity, not professional certification. No match, relationship, job, income or event attendance is guaranteed.

Prices and manual purchases

The current new Premium options are a free 24-hour trial, Weekly at KSh29 for 7 days, and Monthly at KSh99 for 30 days. The free trial is available once per eligible authenticated account and starts only when activated. Paid options do not auto-renew. Existing annual purchases keep their original earned expiry.

Premium includes unlimited Likes, five Super Likes each calendar day in Kenya, inbound likes and discovery priority. Swipe packages (31 Likes for KSh30 and 50 Likes for KSh49) and Nearby access (KSh20 for 24 hours) are separate purchases. The app must show the product and total before an M-Pesa prompt. Safaricom may apply its own transaction charges under its published tariff; those are separate from the Campus Match price. In the Google Play version of the app, purchases are made through Google Play instead, and Google shows the final price and payment options before you pay.

Only confirmed provider results grant a paid entitlement. Pending or unconfirmed payments are not proof of failure: check their status before paying again. We keep checking an unconfirmed payment for up to 7 days. If it is still unconfirmed after that, or the app gives you a support reference, contact support, because some payments need a manual check. Your purchases and any saved M-Pesa receipts are listed in Settings under Purchase History. Early renewal adds the purchased duration after remaining contiguous Premium access. Do not share your M-Pesa PIN with Campus Match. Purchases may be paused or unavailable during maintenance. Only one M-Pesa request can be open on an account at a time, so a new request may be refused for about 3 minutes. After 5 unfinished requests in an hour, new requests may be refused for up to about an hour. Paid requests, and requests that never reached a phone, do not count. These limits help prevent duplicate charges and repeated prompts to someone’s phone. Only enter a phone number you are allowed to use, because the prompt goes to that number. The person paying may be different from the account holder. See the Refund Policy for payment problems.

You can stop using the app, sign out, or request account deletion. Deletion and sign-out do not create or cancel an automatic payment because the reviewed M-Pesa plans do not auto-renew.

The service is provided subject to availability. Nothing in these Terms removes mandatory consumer or data-protection rights. Kenyan law applies subject to any mandatory rights applicable to you. Contact campusmatch2026@gmail.com for support, complaints or questions about these Terms.
