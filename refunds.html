Refund Policy

How to report duplicate, incorrect or unfulfilled purchases.

Updated 7 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-06

On this page

Request a payment review

Email campusmatch2026@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Campus Match refund request”. Include the date, product, amount and M-Pesa transaction reference. If you do not hold the Campus Match account the payment was for, write from your own email address and include the phone number that paid. Share only the information needed to locate the payment. Never send your M-Pesa PIN or account password.

Contact us about a duplicate or unauthorized charge, an incorrect amount, or confirmed payment that did not provide the purchased access. We will review the provider transaction and entitlement records. We check the reference, amount, date and paying number against those records, and we may ask you to show the Safaricom message. Do not make another payment while the first one is unconfirmed.

When a refund applies

We refund the full amount of a duplicate or unauthorized payment confirmed by our records and Safaricom’s. If you were charged more than the displayed price, we refund the difference. If a confirmed payment did not give you the purchased access and we cannot restore it, we refund it.

Access starting immediately does not by itself rule out a refund. For partly used Premium, Likes or Nearby access, we consider what went wrong and what was used, and we may refund the unused part. Simply changing your mind after using a purchase does not normally qualify, unless the law gives you that right.

What happens next

We aim to reply within 5 working days and to finish most reviews within 14 days of receiving what we need. If we are waiting for Safaricom it can take longer, and we will tell you. An approved refund normally goes back to the M-Pesa number that paid. A request is reviewed on its facts. Contacting support does not automatically authorize a refund or guarantee an outcome. A free trial has no charge to refund. Cancelling a future manual purchase has no charge; paid plans have no automatic renewal to cancel.

If a refund is approved, we will explain the amount and the applicable provider process. We do not promise an exact processing time before provider confirmation. Any provider fees or adjustments will be explained during review. These terms do not exclude any refund or remedy required by applicable consumer law.

If you are not satisfied

Reply to our decision and ask for a second review. You can also complain to the Competition Authority of Kenya about consumer issues, or to Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner about personal data.

Account deletion and other payment providers

You may request account deletion without giving up a valid payment complaint. Keep your own receipt or transaction reference. Minimal financial records may be retained for refund, accounting or fraud-review purposes.

This policy describes direct M-Pesa purchases. If you bought in the Google Play version of the app, Google processes the payment and Google Play’s refund process also applies. You can still contact us. If Google refunds or reverses a purchase, the access you bought ends, and purchased Likes are removed from what you have left.

Contact Campus Match

Independent student social service for adults aged 18 or older. Kenya. Not an official Maseno University service.

Support, privacy, refund and deletion requests: campusmatch2026@gmail.com.

Request account deletion by email

Your email app opens. A request is sent only when you send the email. Do not include your password or M-Pesa PIN.
